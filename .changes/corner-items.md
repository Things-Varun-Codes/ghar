Every item in every Our Corner list looks the same and can have a name, notes, a link, a picture and a place. Pasting a link to almost any page fills in its name and picture from what the page itself publishes; Corner no longer knows any site by name. A picture can also be pasted or removed. Pictures are fetched and served by your install, so browsers never contact other sites, and every fetch refuses addresses inside your own network.
