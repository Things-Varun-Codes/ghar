The Timeline is more compact, so far more moments fit on a screen.

- Each moment shows its date, who added it with their face, its title, a line of the story and a small picture. "Details" opens the rest of the story and the photos; tapping anywhere else still opens the moment.
- The place field in a moment works from the keyboard: arrows to choose a suggestion, Enter to pick it, Escape to close the list.
- The picker for linking things into a story groups results by kind and works with the arrow keys. Numbers in a search are now searched for, rather than picking a result.
