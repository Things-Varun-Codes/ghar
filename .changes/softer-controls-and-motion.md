Sheets, menus and other controls have been rebuilt so they behave the same everywhere, and things now move softly when they change.

- On a phone, swipe a sheet down to close it; on a wider screen, swipe it to the right. A sheet with something typed in still asks before throwing it away.
- A page's first rows and doors settle in as it opens. Rows that load later, and pages you come back to, appear in place.
- Lists keep loading as you scroll, and if loading stops, "Try again" picks up where it left off.
- If your device is set to reduce motion, nothing slides or rises; things only fade.
