Expenses has a new look, built around a paper slip like a till receipt, and shows one total: what you owe or are owed. Tapping it opens Balances, where you see each person and settle up.

- The Exchange and Expenses show only your overall total; the Exchange door now shows the same figure as Expenses.
- Balances lists each person you owe or who owes you, with Settle up.
- On Expenses, each group is a card showing where you stand in it and who is in it; a group with nothing owed is stamped Settled.
- A group's slip lists only the payments you make or receive, with Mark paid, then what was spent together and your share. Its "⋯" menu renames, archives or deletes the group from there.
- A group's expenses are listed by month, each with its date, who paid, the amount and your share.
- Tapping an expense or a repayment opens its own page: who shares it, what has happened to it, and a "⋯" menu to edit or delete it. A deleted one can still be opened and restored.
- Adding an expense starts with the amount, set large, and you choose who paid by tapping a face. An equal split says what each person pays. Recording a payment works the same way.
- On a phone, a group's Activity tab shows only the activity.
