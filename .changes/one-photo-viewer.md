A bouquet opens in the same full-screen viewer as photos in Our History, and photos open faster and no longer jump around while the Gallery loads.

- Tapping a photo opens a screen-sized copy instead of the full-size original.
- Gallery tiles hold their square shape while they load.
- Our History no longer loads its other tabs in the background on every visit.
- Gallery thumbnails are sharp on high-resolution screens. Existing photos are redone in the background after the upgrade, and an old blurry thumbnail can show for up to two hours until the browser lets it go.
