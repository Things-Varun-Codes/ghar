A bouquet opens in the same full-screen viewer as photos in Our History, and photos open faster and no longer jump around while the Gallery loads.

- Tapping a photo opens a copy sized for your screen instead of the full-size original, sharp enough to zoom on a large display.
- Gallery tiles hold their square shape while they load.
- Our History no longer loads its other tabs in the background on every visit.
- Gallery thumbnails are sharp on high-resolution screens, while the small pictures on Timeline, Home and On This Day download less. Existing photos are redone in the background after the upgrade, and an old thumbnail can show for up to two hours until the browser lets it go.
