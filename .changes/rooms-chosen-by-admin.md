The admin chooses the rooms. Admin → Rooms turns Our History, Our Corner and Our Mailbox on or off, and turns the Mailbox's notes, flowers, citations and coupons on or off one by one. A room that is off disappears from Home, the phone bar and the install shortcuts, its addresses stop working and it sends no notifications. What was written in it is kept and comes back when it is turned on again.

- Nothing to do on upgrade: an install that already has people keeps every room on.
- A source build that disabled a room in `config/miniapps.php` chooses it in Admin → Rooms instead, so check that page once.
