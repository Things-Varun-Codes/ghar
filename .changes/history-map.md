Our History has a Map tab that shows every moment with a picked place. Tap a pin to open the moment, or choose from the moments at a place you've been to more than once.

- A list of the places sits under the map and works without it.
- The map's tiles come from OpenFreeMap through your own server, so nobody's address reaches the tile host. `MAP_TILES_URL` points it at another host.
