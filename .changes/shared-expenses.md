Our Exchange has a new door, Expenses, for money the household shares. Add what someone paid, pick who it was for, and the page keeps track of who owes whom.

- Make a group for a trip or for something ongoing like groceries; General is there from the start and holds everyone.
- Split an expense equally or type each person's amount.
- Each group shows the fewest payments that settle it, and the Expenses page adds up what you owe and are owed by each person across all your groups, with Settle up for each.
- Anyone in a group can edit, delete or restore its expenses, and the group's Activity tab lists every change with who made it.
- Only the people in a group see its expenses. Notifications and Home say that something was added, never the amount.
- Amounts are shown in the currency of the country `GHAR_TIMEZONE` is in, or the one set by `GHAR_CURRENCY`. Settle both before adding expenses: changing it later relabels the amounts already there, and a currency with a different number of decimals also rescales them.
- Admin → Rooms can turn Expenses off like the other entry types; it is on for homes that already use Exchange.
