Home now shows what needs you and what the household added this week, under today's date, instead of a card for each room.

- **Needs you** pins up citations to pay, coupons someone wants to use, and unread notes and flowers, each on a slip pinned up by the sender's picture, with a button straight to it. Only the person they're for sees them.
- **New this week** lists the moments and list items the others added.
- Birthdays in the next two weeks appear under **Coming up**.
- On a laptop the rooms are now in the sidebar, the same as the bottom bar on a phone.
- Ghar has a new look throughout: a warm white, one easy-to-read typeface, and coral for anything you can press.
- In Our History, each moment on the Timeline shows who posted it as their picture on the line, opens with a tap anywhere on the card instead of a Details button, shows where it happened beside the date, and keeps its photo as a small print. People in Our History are named by first name, as on Home; dates read quietly, and the Gallery's filters, a moment's reactions and the story's formatting buttons each fit on one line on a phone. On a phone the Calendar shows each day's photo and date without a clipped title.
- In Our Shelf, each thing on a list keeps just its done button and a ⋯ menu (which now has Open link), a place no longer repeats its own name, people are named by first name, and a thing that's done says who did it, like "Loved by Asha" (its own page adds the day).
- A list in Our Shelf can let everyone mark things for themselves: turn on "Everyone marks their own" in the list's settings, and Meera's "Loved" no longer counts as yours. Each thing shows who has marked it, and the list's tab becomes "Loved by me". Switching a list either way keeps everything already marked.
