Home now shows what needs you and what the household added this week, under today's date, instead of a card for each room.

- **Needs you** pins up citations to pay, coupons someone wants to use, and unread notes and flowers, each on a slip pinned up by the sender's picture, with a button straight to it. Only the person they're for sees them.
- **New this week** lists the moments and list items the others added.
- Birthdays in the next two weeks appear under **Coming up**.
- On a laptop the rooms are now in the sidebar, the same as the bottom bar on a phone.
- Ghar has a new look throughout: a warm white, one easy-to-read typeface, and coral for anything you can press.
