Our History sends one push each morning with whose birthday it is and what happened on this day in past years.

- It arrives from 9 in the morning, in the household's time (`GHAR_TIMEZONE`), at most once a day, and only to people with Morning memories on in Settings → Notifications. The birthday person gets a happy birthday; everyone else hears whose birthday it is.
- One memory opens that moment; several open the Timeline.
- When you add a moment on someone's birthday, Our History asks whether it is their birthday. A moment linked to a birthday says so on its card, and later mornings remember it: "Meera's birthday, 2022".
- The Timeline and the Calendar both start with "On this day" when something happened on today's date in an earlier year. A moment from 29 February shows on 28 February in other years.
