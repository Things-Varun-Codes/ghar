Our Corner's lists are the household's own. Anyone can make a list, with a name, an icon from the whole lucide set, a word for done (loved, watched, read, cooked or anything) and a heart or a tick. Lists can be renamed and reordered; deleting one with other people's things in it takes an admin.

- The upgrade moves your songs, movies, activities and places into four lists of the same names, with everything kept: who added each one, who marked it done and when, pictures, links, addresses and coordinates.
- Old links, notifications and the links inside History moments keep working.
