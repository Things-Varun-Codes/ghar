Everyone can react to a moment and talk about it underneath.

- Tap ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🙏 or 🎉 in a moment to react, and tap again to take it back. Each reaction shows who left it.
- Comments are plain text, below the photos. You can delete your own; an admin can delete anyone's. A comment you haven't sent yet is kept if you close the moment by mistake: it asks first.
- Whoever added the moment, and everyone who commented before you, gets a push when you comment. It says who commented on which moment, never what they wrote. Reactions send nothing.
