Expenses now have categories, such as Groceries, Eating out, Rent and Taxis, each with its own icon. A group's list can be filtered by category.

- When you add an expense, its category is picked from what you type: "Groceries" gets Groceries, "Taxi" gets Taxis, and a title the group has used before gets the category it had last time. Choosing one yourself always wins.
- The suggestion works offline and in the household's language. When it isn't sure, it leaves the expense without a category.
