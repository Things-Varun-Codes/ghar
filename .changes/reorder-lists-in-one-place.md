Everyone now puts Our Shelf's lists and their expense groups in their own order, and nobody else's changes. "Change order" (in a list's menu, or beside Groups on the Expenses page) opens them all to drag by the handle or move with the arrows.

- Until someone changes it, lists and groups are oldest first, and anything new goes at the bottom. The shelf's old shared order is not kept.
- On Expenses, each balance is one line: who owes whom, the amount, and Settle up.
- On the History timeline, the year and its count of moments share one line, and stay in full view under the tabs as you scroll.
