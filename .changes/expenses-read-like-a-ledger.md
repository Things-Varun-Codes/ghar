Expenses now show where you stand at a glance: an overall total at the top, amounts in green when money comes to you and amber when you owe, and each group's expenses as a dated list, each with its own icon.

- A group's page shows what the group has spent together and your share of it.
- When you add an expense, you can give it an icon from the same set Shelf's lists use, with the usual kinds of spending first.
