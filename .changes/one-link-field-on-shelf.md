Adding to a Shelf list has one field for links now. Paste a map link there and the place's name and picture fill in on their own, and the item's button opens the map.

- The separate Where and Address fields are gone, along with the place line on cards and pages.
- Places you saved before keep where they are: a place with only a map pin now links to that spot on Google Maps, and its address, place name or a second map link moves into its notes.
- Links from more sites now come with their own name and picture.
