Admin → Users now removes someone from the household instead of deleting them. A former member can't sign in and isn't offered anywhere new, but everything they made stays, shown as "Name (deleted)".

- Balances with a former member stay in Expenses and can still be settled.
- Notes, postcards, coupons and citations they shared stay with the other person. Their coupons can't be requested and their citations can't be paid.
- Restore brings them back. Someone who never used Exchange or Shelf can also be deleted for good.
