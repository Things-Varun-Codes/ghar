On Shelf, some pasted links were named with a whole "name · address · rating" line, and that line showed on their open button and under their title.

- The button on an item's page now always reads "Open link".
- A pasted link is named after the page without the site's name ("Cubbon Park", not "Cubbon Park - Wikipedia") and without details after a "·".
- A page that doesn't say what it is, or can't be reached, is named from its link: a map link to Lalbagh Botanical Garden is "Lalbagh Botanical Garden". The name can always be changed.
- Places already saved with the long line under their title show the site instead.
