Everyone can have a profile picture, shown beside their name around the house. Without one, their initials sit in a soft circle instead.

- In Settings → Profile, choose a photo, slide it into the circle and save. You can change or remove it there at any time. An admin can remove anyone's picture from their page in Admin → Users, but only you can put one up.
- Settings → Profile also takes your birthday: the whole date, day, month and year. Once it's set you can change it but not clear it. An admin can set anyone's birthday from their page in Admin → Users.
- Settings → Notifications has a Morning memories switch, on for everyone to start with, for Our History's morning push of birthdays and what happened on this day in past years.
- `GHAR_TIMEZONE` sets where the household lives, so "today" and dates follow your clock rather than the server's or the phone's, down to the day a coupon runs out and the day a comment or a login is shown on. Dates read the way that country writes them unless `GHAR_LOCALE` says otherwise: the order of day and month, the month and weekday names in Our History's calendar, the day its weeks start on, and how long ago a comment was ("5 min ago"). See DEPLOY.md.
- In Our Exchange, a note or bouquet shows the face of who sent it, or of everyone you sent it to, and the people to send to are shown with their faces.
- In Our Shelf, each item shows the face of whoever added it.
