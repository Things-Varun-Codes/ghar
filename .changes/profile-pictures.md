Everyone can have a profile picture, shown beside their name around the house. Without one, their initials sit in a soft circle instead.

- Each person sets or removes their own picture in Settings → Profile; an admin can remove anyone's.
- Settings → Profile also takes a birthday, and Settings → Notifications has a switch for morning memories.
- `GHAR_TIMEZONE` sets where the household lives, so "today" and dates follow your clock rather than the server's. Dates read the way that country writes them unless `GHAR_LOCALE` says otherwise. See DEPLOY.md.
